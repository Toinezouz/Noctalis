# Choosing a licence

## The licence

**AGPL-3.0-or-later** — GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or any
later version. The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

## Why this one

UMBRASTRA is a game **played over a network**. That is exactly the situation
the AGPL was written for, and what sets it apart from the ordinary GPL.

With a plain GPL, someone could take this code, modify it, host it online and
share nothing: they do not *distribute* the software, they give access to it.
Section 13 of the AGPL closes that door — if you run a modified version for
other people, you must offer them the corresponding source code.

In practice, for this project:

- any improvement deployed publicly comes back to the community;
- nobody can turn UMBRASTRA into a closed or paid service;
- the spirit of the project — free, no account, no ads, supported only
  through GitHub Sponsors — is protected by the licence itself, not just by a
  promise.

## What it means for you

| You want to… | Allowed? |
| --- | --- |
| Play | Yes, freely. |
| Read, copy, study the code | Yes. |
| Host it for your friends, as it is | Yes. |
| Modify it for your own private use | Yes, with no obligation to publish. |
| Host a modified version for others | Yes, by publishing your changes under the AGPL. |
| Build it into proprietary software | No. |

## Contributions

By contributing, you agree that your work is released under the same
licence. The project asks for no transfer of rights (no CLA): you stay the
author of your contributions.

## Assets and dependencies

The project's original assets follow the project's licence. The Space Grotesk and
Jost typefaces are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which is compatible.
Details are in [`ASSETS.md`](ASSETS.md).

## Where the code comes from

UMBRASTRA reuses the technical architecture of an earlier personal project by
the same author (rules engine, authoritative server, public/private split,
design system). The identity, texts, drawings and vocabulary of the game are
entirely original and made for this project.
