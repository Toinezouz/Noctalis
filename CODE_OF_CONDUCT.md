# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

NOCTALIS is an open project. We want everyone to be able to take part,
whatever their experience, age, disability, appearance, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, origin, religion or lack of one.

## What we expect

- kindness and respect in every exchange;
- criticism aimed at code, never at people;
- accepting disagreement: several solutions are often better than one;
- patience with people who are just starting out.

## What has no place here

- harassment, in any form;
- sexualised language or imagery;
- personal attacks, insults or discriminatory remarks;
- publishing someone's private information without their explicit consent;
- any behaviour a reasonable person would find out of place in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to every space of the project — issues, pull requests,
discussions — and to situations where someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer, through
the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Toinezouz).
Every report will be looked at seriously and discreetly, and the person who
reports will face no negative consequence for doing so.

The maintainer enforces this code and may remove, edit or reject any
contribution that does not respect it, and ban a contributor temporarily or
permanently.
